About DIRA

Software and digital systems built for real needs.

DIRA SAS is headquartered in Bamenda, Cameroon, and works remotely with clients across all ten regions, as well as with Cameroonians in the diaspora and international clients. We also have satellite hubs in Buea, Douala and Yaoundé. Our work includes websites, digital platforms, workflow automation and tamper-evident records for businesses, institutions and public-sector partners.

DIRA SAS was established in Bamenda to build software and digital services for businesses and institutions.

Our work spans websites, custom platforms, workflow tools and tamper-evident records. We work with clients to understand their needs, develop suitable systems and support delivery.

DIRA SAS also works in institutional partnership with government on regional digital-readiness programmes. It is the software company within the DIRA Group.

Our Approach

From Diagnosis to Ongoing Support

  1. Discovery & Consultation

    We learn how your organization works and diagnose the problem with the people closest to it. We recommend a solution only when it is viable for your needs and constraints.

  2. Build in Focused Sprints

    We develop in manageable stages, check quality as we go and keep you involved through regular reviews so the product stays aligned with the agreed requirements.

  3. Delivery, Adoption & Support

    Launch is not the end. We help your team put the system to use through onboarding and staff training, then provide maintenance and ongoing support.
