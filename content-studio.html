Commercial Production Arm

DIRA Content Studio

DIRA Content Studio provides video production, photography, and branded content services to businesses, institutions, and independent creators. The Studio also produces all content published by the wider DIRA Group, including coverage of DIRA Tech Fest, product media for DHUB, and campaign material for DIRA Foundation, giving it a level of production experience developed initially through internal work before being offered commercially.

Alongside its commercial production work, DIRA Content Studio is a developing a news and media reporting function, covering technology, business, and regional development stories within Bamenda and the wider Northwest Region. This positions the Studio not only as a production service but as an emerging media outlet in its own right, extending DIRA's presence beyond the ventures it builds into the coverage of the sectors it operates in.
