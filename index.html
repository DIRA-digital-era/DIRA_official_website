Who We Are

DIRA is a technology company based in Bamenda, registered under OHADA law. We build software that institutions can actually rely on: records that hold up under audit, platforms that handle real transaction volume, and technical guidance from a team that stays accountable for the outcome.

In 2026 alone, DIRA organized a three-day technology festival that trained 108 young people and drew over 1,000 attendees, launched Cameroon's first verified housing marketplace, and delivered platform work presented directly to senior government officials.
