Project Context & Purpose

Camer Young Boyz FC is a football club in Cameroon dedicated to developing grassroots football talent. Young athletes frequently face challenges being noticed by international scouts without an authoritative online repository documenting verified match statistics, video footage, and roster information.

DIRA developed a responsive web portal allowing the academy to publish match fixtures, player roster cards, and recruitment inquiry pathways directly to international partners.
