Property tools for renters and operators

DHUB brings rental listings (single rooms, studios, apartments, hotels and guest houses) together with property-management tools and booking software for Cameroon.

Individual landlords and owners of multi-tenant properties can manage their properties. University hostels can manage accommodation, while hotels and guest houses can manage bookings.

For individuals, businesses and institutions

  • Rental marketplace: Find rentals (single rooms, studios, apartments, hotels and guest houses) in Cameroon.
  • Landlord and property management: Tools for individual landlords and owners of multi-tenant properties.
  • University accommodation: Aggregated property-management tools for university hostels.
  • Hospitality bookings: Booking tools for hotels and guest houses.
