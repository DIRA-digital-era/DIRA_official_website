The Cross-Border Friction Solved

Cross-border commercial trade across Central and Western Africa faces immense logistical and financial friction. Vendors operating in different currency regimes struggle with real-time exchange rates, delayed bank settlements, and buyer cart abandonment driven by lack of merchant verification.

DIRA was tasked with engineering an institutional platform capable of hosting thousands of merchant catalogs while providing automated merchant identity verification, escrow payment workflows, and unified order fulfillment tracking.

Key Engineering Capabilities Delivered

  • Multi-Tenant Merchant Dashboards: Granular inventory controls, payout ledger analytics, and multi-currency pricing tiers.
  • Escrow Settlement Engine: Automated funds release conditional on verified fulfillment proofs, protecting both buyer and seller.
  • KYC & Merchant Vetting Pipeline: Automated document collection and verification audit trails to prevent marketplace fraud.
  • High-Concurrency Order Processing: Resilient database architecture designed for peak transaction volumes without data inconsistency.
