Regional Digital Readiness Programme

Northwest Digital Readiness Survey

Through the Northwest Digital Readiness Survey (NWDRS), DIRA is working with small and medium-sized enterprises, cooperative financial institutions and social-economy organisations across Cameroon's North West Region to assess their digital needs and readiness.

Programme focus

SMEs, cooperatives and social economy

Geographic scope

North West Region, all seven divisions

Assessment target

30–50 organisations over an estimated eight months

Institutional context

Prepared for the Regional Delegation of MINPMEESA

Estimated programme budget

50–80 million XAF, financed by DIRA

Programme budget and financing

Implementation is estimated at 50,000,000–80,000,000 XAF over eight months, financed by DIRA. MINPMEESA is not asked to contribute to the programme budget.

DIRA may, at its discretion, charge a participating organisation up to 150,000 XAF for its individual assessment and report. Any such fee is communicated before the assessment and is separate from the programme budget.

Digital readiness starts with real needs

Many local organisations manage member or client records on paper, track operations manually and have limited access to digital tools. These constraints can make reporting, day-to-day management and reaching customers or members more difficult.

But adopting technology without understanding how an organisation works can create a different problem: a system that is costly, unsuitable or difficult to sustain. NWDRS assesses existing processes, infrastructure, skills and priorities before identifying what kind of digital change—if any—would be useful.

DIRA is working with cooperative credit unions and microfinance institutions, SMEs, and GICs and other social-economy organisations across the region.

Seven domains, one practical assessment

The assessment combines a structured on-site conversation with a review of an organisation's current tools, workflows and ability to adopt changes. Seven assessment domains help build a rounded picture of readiness:

  • Connectivity and equipmentComputers, internet access, power reliability and data-backup practices.
  • Member and client recordsHow records are stored, maintained and used for reporting.
  • Loan and operational managementHow organisations track loans, workflows and operational performance.
  • Online and digital presenceWebsites, online listings, social channels and digital payment integration.
  • Financial reporting systemsAccounting tools, reporting processes and availability of an audit trail.
  • Staff digital literacyCurrent staff skills, training experience and access to IT support.
  • Governance and adoption readinessTechnology planning, decision-making and capacity to sustain new tools.

Recommendations that fit the organisation

Assessment findings connect business and operational needs with existing digital capacity, staff readiness and practical constraints. The aim is not to prescribe a website or software platform to every participant. For some organisations, the most useful first step may instead be better record-keeping, reliable connectivity, digital skills or a clearer operational process.

This needs-first approach helps organisations and regional stakeholders distinguish between immediate foundational improvements and more advanced digital solutions that could be appropriate later.

Programme work and regional value

DIRA is providing feedback to participating organisations and sharing progress updates while compiling an aggregated Northwest Regional Digital Readiness Index, a final report and priorities for digital, operational and capacity-building support.

Regional findings support evidence-based planning for SME development and digital transformation. The programme aligns with the goals of Cameroon’s National Development Strategy 2020–2030 (SND30), the MINPMEESA strategic framework and relevant regional digital-transformation planning.

Individual organisation assessments are not published on this page; public-facing regional insight is intended to be aggregated and handled with due regard for confidentiality.
