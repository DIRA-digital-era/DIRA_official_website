The Challenge & Problem Solved

SHESA's alumni network spans thousands of professionals across several decades, regional chapters, and international diaspora communities. Historically, managing membership records, tracking infrastructure donations, coordinating elections, and documenting alumni development projects was fragmented across uncoordinated spreadsheets and social messaging groups.

DIRA was commissioned to design and engineer a single centralized platform to serve as the definitive institutional record for the network. The system required high data security, granular role-based access for chapter executives, transparent milestone tracking for alumni-funded development projects, and historical archive preservation.

Key Platform Capabilities

SHESA Connect encompasses four integrated functional pillars:

  • Verified Membership Directory: Member verification by graduation set, chapter, and professional sector.
  • Leadership & Governance Archive: An immutable historical record of past executive councils and chapter leadership.
  • Project Milestone Tracking: Transparent monitoring of alumni-funded development initiatives with progress updates and financial accountability.
  • Events & Media Repository: Centralized event ticketing, attendance logs, and a dedicated archival media gallery.

Institutional Presentations & Recognition
