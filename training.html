Our Methodology

Structured Learning Path

1. Fundamentals & Setup

Every participant masters professional development tooling, version control, and development environments from day one.

2. Hands-On Coding

No passive lectures. Students write, test, and debug real code against realistic production specifications.

3. Capstone Projects

Trainees build and deploy working applications solving actual community or business challenges.

4. Evaluation & Pipeline Placement

DIRA evaluates graduating trainees directly for engineering openings within DIRA and partner organizations.
